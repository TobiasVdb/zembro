# Products hero still life

`products-sunny-table.blend` is a self-contained, editable Blender scene. Its devices, white table, draped linen and studio backdrop are geometry, rendered with Cycles. An off-camera Sun light produces directional shadows, supported by a broad bounce light and sky fill. The web version is `public/assets/products-sunny-table.webp` (2000 × 900).

The Silver Shield geometry is copied from the approved reverse-view scene. Mini, Oktave, Comfort, Wander Alert and the four SafeTrx beacons are illustrative exterior models made against the supplied/official product images in `public/assets`; these are not manufacturer CAD and dimensions are approximate. No product image billboards or painted shadows are used. Watch display details are geometry/text for this still life, not a live product interface.

To rebuild, open `artwork/silver-shield/silver-shield-reverse.blend`, then execute `build_scene.py` through Blender MCP. It creates a new scene and writes only that scene and its dependencies to the new `.blend`, preserving the source scenes. Render with Cycles using the scene camera, then export the PNG to `products-sunny-table.png` and optimize a WebP copy with Sharp at quality 90. The MCP render tool may return a temporary output path; copy that output to this artwork directory.

The revised hero is `products-sunny-table-v2.blend` / `products-sunny-table-v2.png`, built with `refine_scene.py` from the original scene above. It replaces generic thin cube bevels with independently contoured rounded housings. Mini has a shorter, wider shell and deep charging dock; Wander has its Sony face, orange-lined perforated strap, side controls and buckle; Oktave has its cushion case, narrow metal surround, home key and blue clock numerals; Comfort has a charcoal bezel, integrated lugs and digital face. SafeTrx beacons have inset cap seams and branding. Supplied `mini.webp`, `watch.webp`, `comfort.webp` and `wander-alert.webp` are the visual references. Geometry and display marks remain real scene objects; no photographic product planes are used. These are reference-led exterior models, not dimensionally certified CAD.

The current hero is `products-sunny-table-v3.blend` with an independent `products-sunny-table-v3-dark.blend`. Build both by executing `refine_mini.py` with the v2 scene loaded. Mini was rebuilt against the supplied `EV12 Mini²_Frontview.png`, `Sideview.png`, `Backview.png` and `With charging base.png` in `Y:/Zembro/Renders/`. Its front silhouette has a width/height ratio of about 0.56, a shallow domed face and straight side band, seven separate diagonal speaker perforations, a through-housing upper lanyard slot, side keys/indicators, rear fasteners and charging pads. The charging dock tapers outward at its foot and has a recessed well, flush pairing button and vertical status light. The pendant leans back and presents its control side. Earlier scenes and renders remain intact. Models remain approximate exterior geometry.

The dark scene retains the same product materials and camera, with dark teal cloth/backdrop, cool directional light, warm fill and a soft rim. Render each with Cycles and export quality-92 WebP copies to `public/assets/products-sunny-table-v3.webp` and `products-sunny-table-v3-dark.webp`.

The CMS fields `catalog.heroImage`, `heroImageAlt`, `heroImageDark` and `heroImageDarkAlt` control the two hero layers. The website uses the same 450ms opacity crossfade as the Silver Shield scene, with reduced-motion support and only the active layer exposed to keyboard/accessibility APIs. `seed-v10-products-hero` adds initial fields; `seed-v12-products-geometry` upgrades the first render; `seed-v13-mini-dark` upgrades only original/v2 selections (identified by file hashes) and adds missing dark fields in four languages. Custom images, intentionally empty values and translated descriptions are preserved.
